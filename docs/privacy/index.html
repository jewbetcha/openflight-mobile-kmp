OpenFlight Companion privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

OpenFlight Companion is an open-source app for Android and iOS that connects to an OpenFlight golf launch monitor you run yourself. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developers don't run a server and don't receive any of your data.

Your shots

The app receives shot data from your own OpenFlight launch monitor over your local network or Bluetooth, and can send it commands such as the selected club or profile. Shot history, your club bag and your settings are stored only on your phone. They're removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

Location and weather

If you turn on automatic conditions, the app asks for your approximate location and sends it to Open-Meteo over HTTPS to look up the current weather and elevation, which adjust carry distances. The app keeps only the latest weather result, for up to 15 minutes, on your phone. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy covers what it does with requests. If you enter conditions by hand, no location is used or sent.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: to connect to your launch monitor. It's never used to find your location.
  • Local network: to reach your launch monitor on your Wi-Fi.
  • Approximate location: only for automatic weather conditions, as above. On Android 11 and older, Android also requires location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices; the app doesn't read your location for that.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the app's source repository. For questions, open an issue at github.com/open-flight/openflight-mobile-kmp/issues.
